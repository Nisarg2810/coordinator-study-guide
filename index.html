  • Breaking announcements and awards: launches, product news, awards, sometimes urgent statements.

How a big Canadian brand runs bilingual YouTube (a public example)

Many national brands post every video twice: once in English, once in French, often on one channel, sometimes with a separate French Instagram account. Look at a bilingual brand channel before the call and notice:

  • Do English and French titles follow the same pattern?
  • Does every title end with the brand name?
  • Are French titles proofread (spaces, accents)?
  • Does every video have its pair in the other language?
  • How regular are the uploads?
"I looked at how bilingual brands run YouTube. The small things are what make it professional: one title pattern per language, the brand tag on every video, a French proofread, and nothing going live without its pair."

08YouTube Studio, deep dive

YouTube Studio is the back office of a YouTube channel. Everything about a video is set there before it goes live.

Uploading a video, step by step

  1. Get the final, approved file. Not a review version. Check the file name and version number.
  2. Upload in YouTube Studio (Create → Upload videos).
  3. Title: clear, starts with what it is, follows the brand pattern. Example: "All-New Model X | Feature | Brand Canada".
  4. Description: the first two lines matter most (they show in search). Add links and any required legal lines.
  5. Thumbnail: the cover image. Must be readable on a phone, on-brand, the right size (1280×720).
  6. Playlist: group it with similar videos (by product or series).
  7. Audience setting: "not made for kids" for brand ads.
  8. Tags and keywords: help search. Product names, model year, topics.
  9. Captions (subtitles): upload an SRT file in English, and in French for the French version. Check timing and spelling.
  10. End screen and cards: point viewers to the next video or the website.
  11. Visibility: Private (only you), Unlisted (anyone with the link, good for reviews), Public, or Scheduled for a set time.
  12. Final check on a phone, then share the link with the team and log it.

Words to know

  • Metadata: all the information about a video (title, description, tags, category).
  • SRT: a caption file format.
  • Shorts: vertical videos under a minute.
  • Premiere: a scheduled public first showing.
  • Analytics: views, watch time, audience, in YouTube Studio.
"Before any video goes live: approved final file, title, description and tags in both languages, captions in both languages checked, thumbnail readable on a phone, playlist and end screen set, visibility and time confirmed, link shared with the team."

09English and French

  • Why: Canada is bilingual, and Quebec requires French in business communication. National brands publish in both.
  • How it usually works: a French team or translator writes or adapts French text. You don't translate yourself; you make sure the right approved French version goes up.
  • Adapt, don't just translate: French copy is often adapted to sound natural, not word for word.
  • Typography: French puts a space before some punctuation (: ; ! ?) and uses accents (é, è, à). Small errors look sloppy, so proofread.
  • The pair rule: nothing goes live in one language without the other being ready (unless the team decides otherwise).
"I'm not fluent in French, so I'd work closely with the French team, and my checklist makes sure nothing goes live in French without their approval."

10Dealer marketing and the dealer tool

A brand sells through hundreds of local dealerships. Each dealer has its own social pages. The brand wants dealers to post approved, on-brand content, so it gives them a website (a portal) to download ready-made posts.

What you'd do in the dealer tool

  • Upload new approved posts: every size (square, vertical, story) and both languages.
  • Write a short, clear title and description so dealers know what each post is for.
  • Set start and end dates, especially for offers.
  • Remove expired content, so no dealer posts an old price or offer.
  • Tell dealers what's new (a short note or newsletter).

Why it matters

If a dealer posts an expired offer or an old logo, it can mislead customers and hurt the brand. The dealer tool keeps hundreds of local pages consistent.

A holiday campaign starts Monday. On Friday you upload 10 posts (5 sizes × 2 languages) with end dates, check them on the portal, and send dealers a "new this week" note.

Words to know

  • Co-op: money the brand gives dealers to spend on approved local marketing.
  • OEM: the manufacturer (the brand itself).
  • Disclaimer / legal line: small print on offers (prices, terms). Never edit it.

11The asset library (DAM)

DAM means Digital Asset Management: a big searchable library of every final photo, video and ad.

Name every file the same way

BRAND_PRODUCT_CAMPAIGN_ASSET_SIZE_LANG_V#
BRAND_SUV_HOLIDAY_SOCIAL_1080x1350_EN_V3
BRAND_SUV_HOLIDAY_SOCIAL_1080x1350_FR_V3
BRAND_SUV_AWARD_YT-THUMB_1280x720_EN_V1

Tag every file

Brand, product, model year, campaign, platform, size, language, year.

Audit every month

  • Archive old versions (keep only the latest V#).
  • Delete duplicates.
  • Remove expired offers.
  • Check tags are complete.
"One naming rule, required tags, and a monthly audit. Then anyone can find the right file in seconds, in the asset system, the dealer tool and YouTube."

12Urgent requests (breaking news, awards)

  1. Confirm: what exactly, who approves, by when, which channels.
  2. Gather: approved wording and assets (English + French).
  3. Prepare: posts, YouTube if needed, dealer tool if needed.
  4. Approve: get written sign-off.
  5. Publish and check: on a phone, both languages.
  6. Tell the team and log it in the status report.
Never post anything that hasn't been approved, even under time pressure. Ask, don't guess.

★Worked examples: doing the actual tasks

If Sara says "here's a video, what would you do?", this is how to answer, step by step. The brand and product below are made up for practice ("Aurora SUV"); the method is the real one.

Example 1 · "Here's a new 15-second video. Upload it."

You receive: Aurora_SUV_Winter_15s_EN_V3.mp4 and Aurora_SUV_Winter_15s_FR_V3.mp4. The video shows the 2027 Aurora SUV in snow: remote start, heated seats, all-wheel drive.

Step 1 · Find the information (never invent it)

What you needWhere you find it
Is this the approved final?The approval email or project tool (Workfront/Asana). Check the version number matches (V3).
Product name, model year, featuresThe creative brief, the script, the product fact sheet from the client
Approved copy and claimsThe approved copy document. Never write a new claim yourself.
Legal lines (disclaimers)From the account team / client legal. Paste exactly.
French title and descriptionThe French team or translator, adapted and approved
Title and description styleThe channel's recent uploads: copy the same pattern
Keywords people searchYouTube search suggestions (type "2027 Aurora" and see what appears), Google Trends, YouTube Studio's Research tab
Publish date and timeThe content calendar or account lead

Step 2 · Write the metadata (English)

Title (under 70 characters, same pattern as the channel) 2027 Aurora SUV | Ready for Winter | Brand Canada Description (first two lines show in search) Remote start, heated seats and all-wheel drive. The 2027 Aurora SUV is ready for a Canadian winter. Learn more: brand.ca/aurora [Approved legal line, pasted exactly] #Aurora #SUV #BrandCanada Tags 2027 Aurora, Aurora SUV, winter SUV, all-wheel drive SUV, remote start, heated seats, Brand Canada Playlist Aurora SUV · Audience Not made for kids Thumbnail 1280×720, the SUV in snow, product name readable on a phone

Step 3 · The French version (from the French team, you check it)

Titre VUS Aurora 2027 | Prêt pour l’hiver | Marque Canada Description Démarrage à distance, sièges chauffants et traction intégrale. Le VUS Aurora 2027 est prêt pour l’hiver canadien. En savoir plus : marque.ca/aurora [Mention légale approuvée]

What you check (even without being fluent): it came from the French team, it's the approved version, the pattern matches the English title, product names and year are right, and the link goes to the French page. Note: in Canadian French, an SUV is a VUS, and French puts a space before ":".

Step 4 · Captions

  1. Get the final script from the creative team.
  2. Either use YouTube's auto-captions and edit every line, or upload an SRT file the team provides.
  3. Check spelling, product names and timing against the video.
  4. Upload French captions on the French video (from the French team).
What an SRT file looks like 1 00:00:00,500 --> 00:00:03,000 Winter starts early. 2 00:00:03,200 --> 00:00:06,500 Your Aurora starts earlier.

Step 5 · Final settings and QA

"First I'd confirm it's the approved final and find the brief, approved copy and legal lines. I'd write the English metadata following the channel's pattern, get the French from the French team, add and check captions in both languages, set the thumbnail, playlist and end screen, schedule both together, check on a phone, then share the links and log it."

Example 6 · How to find good keywords for a video

  1. Start with the approved product name and model year.
  2. Type it into YouTube search and note the suggestions that appear ("2027 Aurora review", "Aurora winter").
  3. Check Google Trends for the most-searched way to say it.
  4. Look at YouTube Studio's Research tab (what the channel's viewers search for).
  5. Use the words people actually search in the first line of the description and in the tags. Never add claims that aren't approved.
